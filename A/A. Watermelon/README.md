# Problem: Dividing the Watermelon

### problem link: https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/4/A

## Problem Statement

Pete and his friend Billy bought a watermelon and weighed it, and the scales showed the weight `w` kilos. They are fans of even numbers and want to divide the watermelon in such a way that both parts have even weights. They are also extremely tired and want to start eating as soon as possible. 

The challenge is to determine if the watermelon can be divided into two parts with the following conditions:
- Both parts should have an even weight.
- Both parts should have a positive weight.

## Input

The first (and only) input line contains an integer `w` (1 ≤ w ≤ 100) — the weight of the watermelon bought by the boys.

## Output

Print `YES` if the watermelon can be divided into two parts, each of which has an even weight, otherwise print `NO`.

## Examples

### Example 1:

**Input**:
8

**Output**:
"YES"


---

### Example 2:

**Input**:
5

**Output**:
"NO"




## Constraints:
- The weight `w` is between 1 and 100 inclusive.
- You must ensure that both parts of the watermelon have a positive even weight.

### Explanation:

In this problem, we need to check if it is possible to divide the watermelon in such a way that both parts have even weights. The goal is to decide whether the watermelon can be divided into two parts with even weights without any further constraints on the equality of the parts.

Considerations:
1. The weight must be even for a potential division.
2. The watermelon must be divisible in a way that each part has a positive weight.
